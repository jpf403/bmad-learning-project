Color Usage Directions — Schedule Appointment

Four treatments of the same page, differing only in where/how much of the existing "Modern Warmth" palette (primary teal, destructive red, neutral wash, border, white) is applied. No new colors are introduced in any version. All four use the client-confirmed 8px corner rounding on buttons, inputs, and cards — that part is settled and identical across the board. Copy and layout are reused as-is from the current Schedule Appointment mock.

1. Tinted sections 2. Colored accents 3. Bolder header band 4. Combined
Variation 1 of 4
Tinted sections. The booking-form card and each My Appointments row swap their white-fill + thin-border treatment for a light neutral-wash (#EFF6F8) background with no border, so the page reads in distinct zones at a glance instead of thin outlines on white.
