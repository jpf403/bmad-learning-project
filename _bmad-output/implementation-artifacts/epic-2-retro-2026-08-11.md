## What Went Well

- **AD-17's shared-read-path bet paid off directly.** Story 2.5 built `BookingService.GetDaySchedule` generic in `barberId` specifically anticipating Story 2.6 — 2.6 shipped with **zero** service/repository changes, purely a Controller-level branch.
- **`TryCancel`'s atomic conditional-update (Story 2.4) was built once and reused unmodified three times** — customer (2.4), barber (2.5), and admin (2.6) cancellation all call the same race-safe `BookingService.Cancel(appointmentId, callerAccountId, callerRole)` with zero changes at 2.5/2.6.
- **Deferred-item discipline visibly improved over Epic 1.** Per Jack: this epic did a better job addressing deferred items as they came up rather than letting them pile up; the items still open at epic end are genuinely low-priority, not slipped-and-ignored.
- **All four of Epic 1's retro action items were completed, not just attempted** — re-checking `deferred-work.md` at every story kickoff (visible in every story's own Dev Notes), the two-DbContext deterministic race-test pattern (Story 2.1 applied it proactively even before 2.3 needed it, and Story 2.4 extended it to the cancel race), the NavBar overflow fix (landed in Story 2.2, the first UI-touching story), and Story 2.2's explicit Calendar/Select component scoping.

## Challenges

- **Wall-clock/timezone test fragility recurred three times.** Story 2.2 shipped a real midnight-rollover bug in `GetAvailableSlots` because its own test recomputed the same buggy string-comparison as its expected value. Story 2.3 had to retrofit `NextBookableWeekday()` so `BookingControllerTests` wouldn't become a ticking time bomb tied to a hardcoded date. Story 2.6 still hit a live CI flake from `DateTime.Today` reading the GitHub runner's UTC clock instead of AD-12's fixed America/New_York, during the daily UTC/Eastern day-rollover window. Same underlying lesson, three separate incidents.
- **Two stories (2.2, 2.5) needed a second review round because round 1's own fix left a new gap** — 2.2's round-1 in-flight field lock missed the Date/Calendar field; 2.5's round-1 "Try again" retry silently retried against the wrong date after a failed nav.
- **UI placement/layout struggles**, per Jack — addressed as they came up during development (e.g. the admin schedule header's nav-arrow/dropdown layout across two rounds in Story 2.6), but Jack flagged this as an area he may want to revisit again in a dedicated pass near the end of the project rather than considering it fully closed.
- **Stale sprint-status bookkeeping caught mid-epic:** Story 2.5's own Dev Notes noted that the NavBar-overflow action item was still tracked as `open` in `sprint-status.yaml` even though it was resolved back in Story 2.2 — confirmed by reading current `NavBar.css`. Corrected in this retro's sprint-status update.
- **Minor open items, confirmed by Jack as low-priority, not carried forward as hard action items:** no `aria-live` announcement on an admin's barber-switch; no retry affordance on the zero-barbers state; `deferred-work.md` has grown to ten story-sections mixing resolved and open items in one flat list.
