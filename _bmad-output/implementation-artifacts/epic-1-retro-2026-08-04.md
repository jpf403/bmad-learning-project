# Epic 1 Retrospective: Account Access & Site Foundation

**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Participants:** Jack (Project Lead / Product Owner), Amelia (Developer, facilitating), Winston (Architect), Sally (UX Designer), John (PM)

## Epic Summary

- **Delivery:** 7/7 stories done (1.1 Scaffold/CI/Design System, 1.2 Account Entity & Repository, 1.3 Home & About Pages, 1.4 Customer Self-Registration, 1.5 Sign In/Out & First-Admin Bootstrap, 1.6 Server-Side Role Gating & Protected Routing, 1.7 Self-Service Account Management).
- **FRs covered:** FR1-4, FR20-23, FR28, FR29, FR31 — all delivered per the epics document's FR coverage map.
- **Review intensity:** every story went through at least one patch round; Stories 1.2 and 1.7 each needed three. Round count trended upward over the epic rather than down.
- **This is the project's first retrospective** — no prior epic retro to cross-reference.

## What Went Well

- **Test-first rigor caught real (including security) bugs before they shipped.** Jack: review passes cost real effort, but fixed issues that "needed to be solved" — notably security items in Story 1.7 (current-password re-verification, extending rate-limiting to password changes).
- **"Build infrastructure with zero consumers, next story adopts it for free"** — `SessionLivenessMiddleware`/`RequireRole`, built in Story 1.6 with no caller, consumed by Story 1.7 unmodified.
- **The Auth domain trio** (`AuthController` → `AuthService` → `AccountRepository`), started in Story 1.4, extended cleanly through Stories 1.5 and 1.6 with zero rewrites.
- **`SqliteApiFactory`** (built in Story 1.2) was reused verbatim as the test fixture for every subsequent story — no fixture churn across the epic.
- **"Wire the route now, build the destination later"** — established in Story 1.3 (Home CTA → `/login` before `/login` existed) — was reused explicitly by name in Stories 1.4, 1.5, and 1.6.

## Challenges

- **NavBar overflow below ~640px** has been broken since Story 1.1, flagged in Story 1.3's review, assigned to Story 1.5, and left untouched through 1.5, 1.6, and 1.7. Jack's read: "one of those things that just slipped by" — a handoff gap, not a testing gap. **Decision: fix during Epic 2**, since Epic 2 adds new pages that inherit the same bug.
- **CRLF/Prettier false-positives** from Windows `core.autocrlf` were independently rediscovered in Stories 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, and 1.6 before 1.6 fixed the root cause with `.gitattributes`. Jack's read: unlike NavBar, this was a deliberate scope-skip each time until the annoyance outweighed deferring it again.
- **Deferred work isn't being re-checked at story kickoff.** `deferred-work.md` is written to faithfully but nothing forces the next story to read it back — Story 1.4 even re-deferred an item Story 1.3 had already flagged, instead of picking it up.
- Lower-severity open items carried in `deferred-work.md`: unverified SQLitePCLRaw CVE-2025-6965 fix, no logging (`ILogger`) anywhere in `AuthController`/`AccountController`, case-sensitive rate-limit partition key, untested DB-constraint-race backstop in `AuthService`.

## Key Insight

Epic 2's core mechanism — the double-booking guard (Story 2.3, FR9/FR10) — is structurally identical to the untested DB-constraint race backstop flagged as debt in Story 1.4 (app-level check-then-insert + DB-level partial unique index backstop, "inherently hard to test deterministically without mocking, which AD-4 disallows"). Surfacing this now avoids re-discovering the same testing wall mid-epic, at higher stakes since it's Epic 2's central value proposition rather than a side concern.
